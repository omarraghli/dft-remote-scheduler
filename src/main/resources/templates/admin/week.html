What the two boxes do

remote is a wish. The schedule grants as many as the week has room for, and when more people want a day than it has slots, who gets it is down to the draw. Nobody is worse off for asking.

bureau is a requirement, and the only one of the two that can fail. Somebody held in the office on Lundi and Vendredi is left Mardi to Jeudi, which is three days in a row and against the rules — that save is refused rather than left to surface on the morning the week is due. A week with a férié in it runs out of room one day sooner. Everyone gets 3 remote days this week.
